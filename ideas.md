# IWACU OUTFITS — Design Direction

## Three initial directions

### Theme Name: Monochrome Atelier
Very Brief Intro: A refined black, bone, and tobacco fashion system with editorial typography, tactile texture, and gallery-like spacing. It makes streetwear feel collected, considered, and premium.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Kigali After Dark

## Chosen direction: Monochrome Atelier

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial minimalism with references to independent fashion lookbooks, art-book layouts, and the disciplined visual language of luxury maisons.

### Core Principles
1. Treat every product image as an editorial object, not a thumbnail.
2. Use asymmetry, generous negative space, and decisive type scale to create confidence.
3. Keep interaction motion quiet, physical, and purposeful: hover reveals, slide-in panels, and soft fades instead of flashy effects.
4. Let material contrast do the work: matte bone surfaces, ink-black type, grain, paper-like texture, and occasional chrome details.

### Color Philosophy
The palette is grounded in ink black (#11110F), warm bone (#F3F0E9), mineral taupe (#B7B0A4), and a signature burnt saffron (#C96D3B). Black communicates authority, bone keeps the interface breathable, taupe adds tactile warmth, and saffron acts as a human, memorable signal for newness and action. The accent is used sparingly so it feels ownable rather than decorative.

### Layout Paradigm
A magazine-like vertical rhythm built from full-bleed image moments, offset text blocks, narrow metadata rails, and uneven product groupings. The page should feel composed rather than uniformly tiled; the product grid can remain functional while the surrounding sections create visual pacing.

### Signature Elements
1. Thin editorial rules and numbered section markers that make the page feel like a printed lookbook.
2. Saffron micro-labels for New, Edit, and WhatsApp ordering moments.
3. Oversized italic pull quotes and image captions that behave like marginalia.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should reward attention. Product cards shift slightly, reveal quick actions, and expose editorial metadata. Drawers and modals should enter with a confident slide and leave quickly. Every action gives clear feedback without interrupting browsing.

### Animation
Use 180–320ms transitions with a strong ease-out curve. Reveal page sections through IntersectionObserver with slight upward movement and opacity changes. Product media may scale to 1.035 on hover. The hero should use a slow, barely visible image drift, while the cart drawer uses a clean horizontal slide. Always respect prefers-reduced-motion.

### Typography System
Use Cormorant Garamond for display headlines and editorial quotes, Space Grotesk for navigation, labels, prices, and interface controls, and a slight uppercase tracking treatment for metadata. Display headlines should be large and compact; body copy should be restrained, with a 1.55 line height and a max measure around 52rem.

### Brand Essence
IWACU OUTFITS is a Rwandan fashion edit for people who dress with intention, bringing elevated streetwear and everyday essentials together in one confident visual world. Personality: assured, tactile, observant.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are concise and declarative. CTAs sound like invitations into a point of view, not generic ecommerce prompts. Microcopy is warm, precise, and lightly editorial.

Example lines: “Wear the part of you that does not need explaining.” “The edit is small. Your point of view is not.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark is a wide, custom-feeling uppercase lockup with generous tracking, paired with a small four-point “I” monogram built from two crossing garment seams. The mark should be used as a simple, bold symbol in the header and favicon rather than a text logo alone.

### Signature Brand Color
Burnt saffron — #C96D3B.
